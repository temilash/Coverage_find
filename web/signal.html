Why is my signal bad here?

Enter a postcode or click the map. Coverage Detective gathers evidence for that exact point from public data (through Zetaris) and live sources: measured speeds, the nearest site of each network and whether hills block the line to it, area figures and current conditions.

The evidence is computed from data. "Diagnose with agents" then asks a small team of agents (NVIDIA Nemotron) to rank the likely causes, check each other's work and explain it.
